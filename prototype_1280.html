Project 3 & Origin Story

The Tale of haX.baby & Pip 150-WORD ORIGIN

Deep inside the forgotten dial-up nodes of the late nineties, when CRT monitors hummed with gentle static, a lone digital dreamer named Pip awoke. Surrounded by neon banners, blinking marquees, and endless endless scroll feeds that drained human souls, Pip sought refuge in the quiet hours between midnight and dawn.

Pip gathered discarded vector stars, wooden lullaby gears, and vintage bedtime cloches to build haX.baby: an enclave where interfaces honor rest instead of endless metrics. Here on the nightstand, every corner is finite, zero-scroll, and intentionally soothing. No doom-scrolling, no algorithmic urgency—just a warm glowing cloche, an honest song, and starry skies.

Interactive Nightstand Guide

1. Zero-Scroll Strictness: Everything fits within 100dvh without page scrollbars.

3. Day/Night Pull Cord: Tug the brass beaded tassel in top right to toggle between Warm Cream (#fff8f1) and Deep Berry/Espresso (#1a1014).
